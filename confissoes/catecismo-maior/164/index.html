Pergunta 164

How many sacraments hath Christ instituted in his church under the New Testament?

Under the New Testament Christ hath instituted in his church only two sacraments, Baptism and the Lord's supper.1

  1. 1 Mateus 26:26-28; Mateus 28:19; 1 Coríntios 11:20; 1 Coríntios 11:23E enquanto comiam, Jesus tomou o pão, abençoou-o, e o partiu. Então o deu aos discípulos, e disse: Tomai, comei; isto é o meu corpo.
