Of Baptism

Confissão de Fé de Westminster, capítulo 28

28.1

Baptism is a sacrament of the New Testament, ordained by Jesus Christ,1 not only for the solemn admission of the party baptized into the visible Church,2 but also to be unto him a sign and seal of the covenant of grace,3 of his ingrafting into Christ,4 of regeneration,5 of remission of sins,6 and of his giving up unto God, through Jesus Christ, to walk in newness of life:7 which sacrament is, by Christ's own appointment, to be continued in his Church until the end of the world.8

28.5

Although it be a great sin to contemn or neglect this ordinance,1 yet grace and salvation are not so inseparably annexed unto it, as that no person can be regenerated or saved without it,2 or that all that are baptized are undoubtedly regenerated.3

  1. 1 Lucas 7:30; Êxodo 4:24-26Mas os fariseus e os estudiosos da Lei rejeitaram o conselho de Deus contra si mesmos, e não foram batizados por ele.
  2. 2 Atos 10:2; Atos 10:4; Atos 10:22; Atos 10:31; Atos 10:45; Atos 10:47; Romanos 4:11Devoto, e temente a Deus, com toda a sua casa; e que fazia muitas doações ao povo, e continuamente orava a Deus.
  3. 3 Atos 8:13; Atos 8:23E até mesmo Simão creu; e tendo sido batizado, ele continuou com Filipe; e vendo os sinais e maravilhas que eram feitas, ele ficou admirado.

28.6

The efficacy of baptism is not tied to that moment of time wherein it is administered;1 yet, notwithstanding, by the right use of this ordinance the grace promised is not only offered, but really exhibited and conferred by the Holy Ghost, to such (whether of age or infants) as that grace belongeth unto, according to the counsel of God's own will, in his appointed time.2

  1. 1 João 3:5; João 3:8Respondeu Jesus: Em verdade, em verdade te digo, que aquele que não nascer de água e do Espírito, não pode entrar no Reino de Deus.
