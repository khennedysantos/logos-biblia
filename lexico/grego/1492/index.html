estar ciente, contemplar, poder (não dizer), considerar, (ter) saber (conhecimento), olhar (para), perceber, ver, estar certo, dizer, entender, desejar, saber

Definição original (inglês)

used only in certain past tenses, the others being borrowed from the equivalent G3700 (ὀπτάνομαι) and G3708 (ὁράω); properly, to see (literally or figuratively); by implication, (in the perfect tense only) to know
KJV: be aware, behold, X can (+ not tell), consider, (have) know(-ledge), look (on), perceive, see, be sure, tell, understand, wish, wot
