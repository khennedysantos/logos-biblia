râgalH7270

Estudar no Sola: Gênesis 42:9

Definição original (inglês)

to walk along; but only in specifically, applications, to reconnoiter, to be atale-bearer (i.e. slander); to lead about
KJV: backbite, search, slander, (e-) spy (out), teach to go, view.
