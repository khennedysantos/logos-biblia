Isaías 41:28

E olhei, porém ninguém havia. Até entre estes nenhum conselheiro havia a quem eu perguntasse, e ele me respondesse algo.

KJV For I beheld, and there was no man; even among them, and there was no counsellor, that, when I asked of them, could answer a word.

RVA Miré, y no había ninguno; y pregunté de estas cosas, y ningún consejero hubo: preguntéles, y no respondieron palabra.
