Isaías 62:10

Passai! Passai pelas portas! Preparai o caminho ao povo! Aplanai! Aplanai a estrada, limpai -a das pedras! Levantai uma bandeira aos povos!

KJV Go through, go through the gates; prepare ye the way of the people; cast up, cast up the highway; gather out the stones; lift up a standard for the people.

RVA Pasad, pasad por las puertas; barred el camino al pueblo; allanad, allanad la calzada, quitad las piedras, alzad pendón á los pueblos.
