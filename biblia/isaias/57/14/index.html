Isaías 57:14

E será dito: Aplanai! Aplanai! Preparai o caminho! Tirai os tropeços do caminho do meu povo!

KJV And shall say, Cast ye up, cast ye up, prepare the way, take up the stumblingblock out of the way of my people.

RVA Y dirá: Allanad, allanad; barred el camino, quitad los tropiezos del camino de mi pueblo.
