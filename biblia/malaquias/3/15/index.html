Malaquias 3:15

Chamemos de sortudos os soberbos, pois os que praticam perversidade prosperam; mesmo tentando a Deus, eles escapam.

KJV And now we call the proud happy; yea, they that work wickedness are set up; yea, they that tempt God are even delivered.
