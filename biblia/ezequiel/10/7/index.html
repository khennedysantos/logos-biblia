Ezequiel 10:7

E um querubim estendeu sua mão dentre os querubins ao fogo que estava entre os querubins, e tomou-o, e o deu nas mãos do que estava vestido de linho; o qual o tomou, e saiu.

KJV And one cherub stretched forth his hand from between the cherubims unto the fire that was between the cherubims, and took thereof, and put it into the hands of him that was clothed with linen: who took it, and went out.

RVA Y un querubín extendió su mano de entre los querubines al fuego que estaba entre los querubines, y tomó, y puso en las palmas del que estaba vestido de lienzos, el cual lo tomó y salióse.
