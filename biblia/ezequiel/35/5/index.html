Ezequiel 35:5

Porque tiveste inimizade perpétua, e fizeste os filhos de Israel serem dispersos pelo poder da espada no tempo de sua aflição, no tempo do castigo final.

KJV Because thou hast had a perpetual hatred, and hast shed the blood of the children of Israel by the force of the sword in the time of their calamity, in the time that their iniquity had an end:

RVA Por cuanto tuviste enemistades perpetuas, y esparciste los hijos de Israel á poder de espada en el tiempo de su aflicción, en el tiempo extremadamente malo;
