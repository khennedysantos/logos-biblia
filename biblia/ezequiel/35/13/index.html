Ezequiel 35:13

Assim vos engradecestes contra mim com vossa boca, e multiplicastes vossas palavras contra mim. Eu ouvi.

KJV Thus with your mouth ye have boasted against me, and have multiplied your words against me: I have heard them.

RVA Y os engrandecisteis contra mí con vuestra boca, y multiplicasteis contra mí vuestras palabras. Yo lo oí.
