Ezequiel 36:36

Então as nações que restarem ao vosso redor saberão que eu, o SENHOR, reedifico as cidades destruídas, e replanto as assoladas; eu, o SENHOR, falei e farei.

KJV Then the heathen that are left round about you shall know that I the Lord build the ruined places, and plant that that was desolate: I the Lord have spoken it, and I will do it.

RVA Y las gentes que fueron dejadas en vuestros alrededores, sabrán que yo edifiqué las derribadas, y planté las asoladas: yo Jehová he hablado, y harélo.
