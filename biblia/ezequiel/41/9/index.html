Ezequiel 41:9

A largura da parede das câmaras externas era de cinco côvados, e o espaço que restava era o lugar das câmaras laterais que eram junto ao templo.

KJV The thickness of the wall, which was for the side chamber without, was five cubits: and that which was left was the place of the side chambers that were within.

RVA Y la anchura de la pared de afuera de las cámaras era de cinco codos, y el espacio que quedaba de las cámaras de la casa por de dentro.
