Ezequiel 33:22

Ora, a mão do SENHOR estivera sobre mim à tarde, antes que o escapado viesse, e abrira minha boca, até que chegou a mim pela manhã; e minha boca se abriu, e nunca mais fiquei mudo.

KJV Now the hand of the Lord was upon me in the evening, afore he that was escaped came; and had opened my mouth, until he came to me in the morning; and my mouth was opened, and I was no more dumb.

RVA Y la mano de Jehová había sido sobre mí la tarde antes que el escapado viniese, y había abierto mi boca, hasta que vino á mí por la mañana; y abrió mi boca, y no más estuve callado.
