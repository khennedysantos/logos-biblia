Ezequiel 26:7

Porque assim diz o Senhor DEUS: Eis que desde o norte eu trarei contra Tiro a Nabucodonosor, rei da Babilônia, rei de reis, com cavalos, carruagens, cavaleiros, tropas, e muito povo.

KJV For thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I will bring upon Tyrus Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, a king of kings, from the north, with horses, and with chariots, and with horsemen, and companies, and much people.

RVA Porque así ha dicho el Señor Jehová: He aquí que del aquilón traigo yo contra Tiro á Nabucodonosor, rey de Babilonia, rey de reyes, con caballos, y carros, y caballeros, y compañías, y mucho pueblo.
