Ezequiel 26:5

Servirá para estender redes no meio do mar, porque assim eu falei,diz o Senhor DEUS; e será saqueada pelas nações.

KJV It shall be a place for the spreading of nets in the midst of the sea: for I have spoken it, saith the Lord God: and it shall become a spoil to the nations.

RVA Tendedero de redes será en medio de la mar, porque yo he hablado, dice el Señor Jehová: y será saqueada de las gentes.
