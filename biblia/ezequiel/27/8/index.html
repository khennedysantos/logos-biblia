Ezequiel 27:8

Os moradores de Sidom e de Arvade eram teus remadores; teus sábios, ó Tiro, que estavam em ti, eles foram teus pilotos.

KJV The inhabitants of Zidon and Arvad were thy mariners: thy wise men, O Tyrus, that were in thee, were thy pilots.

RVA Los moradores de Sidón y de Arvad fueron tus remeros: tus sabios, oh Tiro, estaban en ti; ellos fueron tus pilotos.
