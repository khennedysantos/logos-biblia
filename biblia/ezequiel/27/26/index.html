Ezequiel 27:26

Teus remadores te trouxeram a muitas águas; o vento oriental te quebrou no meio dos mares.

KJV Thy rowers have brought thee into great waters: the east wind hath broken thee in the midst of the seas.

RVA En muchas aguas te engolfaron tus remeros: viento solano te quebrantó en medio de los mares.
