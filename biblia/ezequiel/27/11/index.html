Ezequiel 27:11

Os filhos de Arvade e teu exército estavam sobre teus muros ao redor, e os gamaditas em tuas torres; penduravam seus escudos sobre teus muros ao redor; eles aperfeiçoavam tua beleza.

KJV The men of Arvad with thine army were upon thy walls round about, and the Gammadims were in thy towers: they hanged their shields upon thy walls round about; they have made thy beauty perfect.

RVA Y los hijos de Arvad con tu ejército estuvieron sobre tus muros alrededor, y los Gammadeos en tus torres: sus escudos colgaron sobre tus muros alrededor; ellos completaron tu hermosura.
