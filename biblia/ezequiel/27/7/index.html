Ezequiel 27:7

Linho bordado do Egito era tua cortina, para te servir de vela; de azul e púrpura das ilhas de Elisá era teu toldo.

KJV Fine linen with broidered work from Egypt was that which thou spreadest forth to be thy sail; blue and purple from the isles of Elishah was that which covered thee.

RVA De fino lino bordado de Egipto fué tu cortina, para que te sirviese de vela; de cárdeno y grana de las islas de Elisah fué tu pabellón.
