Ezequiel 27:6

Fizeram teus remos com carvalhos de Basã; fizeram teus bancos com ciprestes das ilhas do Chipre, unidos com marfim.

KJV Of the oaks of Bashan have they made thine oars; the company of the Ashurites have made thy benches of ivory, brought out of the isles of Chittim.

RVA De castaños de Basán hicieron tus remos: compañía de Asirios hicieron tus bancos de marfil de las islas de Chittim.
