Ezequiel 34:11

Porque assim diz o Senhor DEUS: Eis que eu, eu mesmo, procurarei minhas ovelhas, e as buscarei.

KJV For thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I, even I, will both search my sheep, and seek them out.

RVA Porque así ha dicho el Señor Jehová: He aquí, yo, yo requeriré mis ovejas, y las reconoceré.
