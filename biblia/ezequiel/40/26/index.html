Ezequiel 40:26

E suas subidas eram de sete degraus, com seus alpendres diante deles; e tinha palmeiras de um lado e do outro em seus pilares.

KJV And there were seven steps to go up to it, and the arches thereof were before them: and it had palm trees, one on this side, and another on that side, upon the posts thereof.

RVA Y sus gradas eran de siete peldaños, con sus arcos delante de ellas; y tenía palmas, una de una parte, y otra de la otra, en sus postes.
