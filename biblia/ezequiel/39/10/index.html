Ezequiel 39:10

E não trarão lenha do campo, nem a cortarão dos bosques; em vez disso, queimarão as armas no fogo; e tomarão daqueles que deles tomaram, despojarão aos que os despojaram, diz o Senhor DEUS.

KJV So that they shall take no wood out of the field, neither cut down any out of the forests; for they shall burn the weapons with fire: and they shall spoil those that spoiled them, and rob those that robbed them, saith the Lord God.

RVA Y no traerán leña del campo, ni cortarán de los bosques, sino que quemarán las armas en el fuego: y despojarán á sus despojadores, y robarán á los que los robaron, dice el Señor Jehová.
