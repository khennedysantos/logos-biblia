Marcos 5:33

Então a mulher temendo, e tremendo, sabendo o que lhe havia sido feito, veio, prostrou-se diante dele, e disse-lhe toda a verdade.

KJV But the woman fearing and trembling, knowing what was done in her, came and fell down before him, and told him all the truth.
