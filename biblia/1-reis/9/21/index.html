1 Reis 9:21

A seus filhos que restaram na terra depois deles, que os filhos de Israel não puderam acabar, fez Salomão que servissem com tributo até hoje.

KJV Their children that were left after them in the land, whom the children of Israel also were not able utterly to destroy, upon those did Solomon levy a tribute of bondservice unto this day.

RVA A sus hijos que quedaron en la tierra después de ellos, que los hijos de Israel no pudieron acabar, hizo Salomón que sirviesen con tributo hasta hoy.
