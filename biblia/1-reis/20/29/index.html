1 Reis 20:29

Sete dias tiveram assentado o acampamento uns diante dos outros, e ao sétimo dia começou a batalha: e mataram os filhos de Israel dos sírios em um dia cem mil homens a pé.

KJV And they pitched one over against the other seven days. And so it was, that in the seventh day the battle was joined: and the children of Israel slew of the Syrians an hundred thousand footmen in one day.

RVA Siete días tuvieron asentado campo los unos delante de los otros, y al séptimo día se dió la batalla: y mataron los hijos de Israel de los Siros en un día cien mil hombres de á pie.
