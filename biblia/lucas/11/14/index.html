Lucas 11:14

E Jesus estava expulsando um demônio, e este era mudo. E aconteceu que, saindo o demônio, o mudo falou, e as multidões se maravilharam.

KJV And he was casting out a devil, and it was dumb. And it came to pass, when the devil was gone out, the dumb spake; and the people wondered.

RVA Y estaba él lanzando un demonio, el cual era mudo: y aconteció que salido fuera el demonio, el mudo habló, y las gentes se maravillaron.
