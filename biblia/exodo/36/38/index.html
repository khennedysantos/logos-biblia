Êxodo 36:38

E suas cinco colunas com seus capitéis: e cobriu as cabeças delas e suas molduras de ouro: mas suas cinco bases as fez de bronze.

KJV And the five pillars of it with their hooks: and he overlaid their chapiters and their fillets with gold: but their five sockets were of brass.

RVA Y sus cinco columnas con sus capiteles: y cubrió las cabezas de ellas y sus molduras de oro: pero sus cinco basas las hizo de metal.
