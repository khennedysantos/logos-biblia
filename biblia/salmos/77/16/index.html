Salmos 77:16

As águas te viram, ó Deus; as águas te viram, e tremeram; também os abismos foram abalados.

KJV The waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee; they were afraid: the depths also were troubled.

RVA Viéronte las aguas, oh Dios; viéronte las aguas, temieron; y temblaron los abismos.
