Salmos 80:1

Para o regente, conforme “Susanedute”. Samo de Asafe: Ó Pastor de Israel, inclina teus ouvidos a mim,tu que pastoreias a José como a ovelhas, que habitas entre os querubins, mostra teu brilho,

KJV To the chief Musician upon Shoshannim–Eduth, A Psalm of Asaph. Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, thou that leadest Joseph like a flock; thou that dwellest between the cherubims, shine forth.
