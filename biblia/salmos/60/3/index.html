Salmos 60:3

Mostraste ao teu povo coisas duras; nos fizeste beber vinho perturbador.

KJV Thou hast shewed thy people hard things: thou hast made us to drink the wine of astonishment.

RVA Has hecho ver á tu pueblo duras cosas: hicístenos beber el vino de agitación.
