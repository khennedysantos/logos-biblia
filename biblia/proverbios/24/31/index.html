Provérbios 24:31

E eis que ela estava toda cheia de espinheiros, e sua superfície coberta de urtigas; e sua cerca de pedra estava derrubada.

KJV And, lo, it was all grown over with thorns, and nettles had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall thereof was broken down.
