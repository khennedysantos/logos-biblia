Jeremias 52:18

Tomaram também os caldeirões, as pás, os cortadores de pavios, as bacias, os pratos, e todos os vasos de bronze com que faziam o serviço no templo;

KJV The caldrons also, and the shovels, and the snuffers, and the bowls, and the spoons, and all the vessels of brass wherewith they ministered, took they away.

RVA Lleváronse también los calderos, y los badiles, y los salterios, y las bacías, y los cazos, y todos los vasos de metal con que se servían.
