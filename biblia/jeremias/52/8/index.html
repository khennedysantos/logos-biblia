Jeremias 52:8

Porém o exército dos caldeus perseguiu o rei, e alcançaram a Zedequias nas planícies de Jericó; e todo o seu exército se dispersou dele.

KJV But the army of the Chaldeans pursued after the king, and overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho; and all his army was scattered from him.
