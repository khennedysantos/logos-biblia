Jeremias 52:19

E o capitão da guarda tomou os copos, os incensários, as bacias, as panelas, os castiçais, os pratos, e vasos de ofertas de líquidos: tudo o que era de ouro ou de prata.

KJV And the basons, and the firepans, and the bowls, and the caldrons, and the candlesticks, and the spoons, and the cups; that which was of gold in gold, and that which was of silver in silver, took the captain of the guard away.

RVA Y las copas, é incensarios, y tazones, y ollas, y candeleros, y escudillas, y tazas: lo que de oro de oro, y lo que de plata de plata, se llevó el capitán de la guardia.
