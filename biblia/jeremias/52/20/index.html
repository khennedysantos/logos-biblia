Jeremias 52:20

As duas colunas, o mar, e os doze bois de bronze que estavam debaixo das bases, que o rei Salomão tinha feito na casa do SENHOR. não se podia pesar o bronze de todos estes vasos.

KJV The two pillars, one sea, and twelve brasen bulls that were under the bases, which king Solomon had made in the house of the Lord: the brass of all these vessels was without weight.

RVA Las dos columnas, un mar, y doce bueyes de bronce que estaban debajo de las basas, que había hecho el rey Salomón en la casa de Jehová: no se podía pesar el metal de todos estos vasos.
