Jeremias 48:19

Para-te no caminho, e olha, ó moradora de Aroer; pergunta ao que vai fugindo, e à que escapou, dize: O que aconteceu?

KJV O inhabitant of Aroer, stand by the way, and espy; ask him that fleeth, and her that escapeth, and say, What is done?
