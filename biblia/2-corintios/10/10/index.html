2 Coríntios 10:10

Porque eles dizem que as cartas são em verdade graves e fortes, mas a presença do corpo é fraca, e a palavra desprezível.

KJV For his letters, say they, are weighty and powerful; but his bodily presence is weak, and his speech contemptible.
