Neemias 9:25

E tomaram cidades fortificadas e terra fértil, e tomaram posse de casas cheias de toda fartura, cisternas cavadas, vinhas e olivais, e muitas árvores frutíferas; e comeram, se fartaram, engordaram, e se deleitaram pela tua grande bondade.

KJV And they took strong cities, and a fat land, and possessed houses full of all goods, wells digged, vineyards, and oliveyards, and fruit trees in abundance: so they did eat, and were filled, and became fat, and delighted themselves in thy great goodness.

RVA Y tomaron ciudades fortalecidas, y tierra pingüe, y heredaron casas llenas de todo bien, cisternas hechas, viñas y olivares, y muchos árboles de comer; y comieron, y hartáronse, y engrosáronse, y deleitáronse en tu grande bondad.
