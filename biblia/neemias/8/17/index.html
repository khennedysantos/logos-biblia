Neemias 8:17

E toda a congregação dos que voltaram do cativeiro fizeram tendas, e em tendas habitaram; porque os filhos de Israel, desde os dias de Josué filho de Num até aquele dia, não haviam feito assim. E houve alegria muito grande.

KJV And all the congregation of them that were come again out of the captivity made booths, and sat under the booths: for since the days of Jeshua the son of Nun unto that day had not the children of Israel done so. And there was very great gladness.

RVA Y toda la congregación que volvió de la cautividad hicieron cabañas, y en cabañas habitaron; porque desde los días de Josué hijo de Nun hasta aquel día, no habían hecho así los hijos de Israel. Y hubo alegría muy grande.
