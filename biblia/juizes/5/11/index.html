Juízes 5:11

Longe do ruído dos arqueiros, nos bebedouros, Ali repetirão as justiças do SENHOR, As justiças de suas vilas em Israel; Então o povo do SENHOR desceu às portas.

KJV They that are delivered from the noise of archers in the places of drawing water, there shall they rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord, even the righteous acts toward the inhabitants of his villages in Israel: then shall the people of the Lord go down to the gates.

RVA Lejos del ruido de los archeros, en los abrevaderos, allí repetirán las justicias de Jehová, las justicias de sus villas en Israel; entonces bajará el pueblo de Jehová á las puertas.
