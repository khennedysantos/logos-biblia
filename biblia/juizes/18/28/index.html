Juízes 18:28

E não houve quem os defendesse, porque estavam longe de Sidom, e não tinham comércio com ninguém. E a cidade estava no vale que há em Bete-Reobe. Logo reedificaram a cidade, e habitaram nela.

KJV And there was no deliverer, because it was far from Zidon, and they had no business with any man; and it was in the valley that lieth by Beth–rehob. And they built a city, and dwelt therein.

RVA Y no hubo quien los defendiese, porque estaban lejos de Sidón, y no tenían comercio con nadie. Y la ciudad estaba en el valle que hay en Beth-rehob. Luego reedificaron la ciudad, y habitaron en ella.
