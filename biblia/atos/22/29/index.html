Atos 22:29

Então logo se afastaram dele aqueles que estavam para interrogá-lo; e até o comandante teve temor, ao entender que Paulo era romano, e que tinha o atado.

KJV Then straightway they departed from him which should have examined him: and the chief captain also was afraid, after he knew that he was a Roman, and because he had bound him.

RVA Así que, luego se apartaron de él los que le habían de atormentar: y aun el tribuno también tuvo temor, entendido que era Romano, por haberle atado.
