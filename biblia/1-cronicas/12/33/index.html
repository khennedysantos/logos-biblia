1 Crônicas 12:33

E de Zebulom, cinquenta mil, que saíam como exército preparados para a batalha, com todas as armas de guerra; capazes de batalhar com firmeza de coração.

KJV Of Zebulun, such as went forth to battle, expert in war, with all instruments of war, fifty thousand, which could keep rank: they were not of double heart.
