2 Reis 19:18

E que puseram no fogo a seus deuses, porquanto eles não eram deuses, mas sim obra de mãos de homens, madeira ou pedra, e assim os destruíram.

KJV And have cast their gods into the fire: for they were no gods, but the work of men’s hands, wood and stone: therefore they have destroyed them.

RVA Y que pusieron en el fuego á sus dioses, por cuanto ellos no eran dioses, sino obra de manos de hombres, madera ó piedra, y así los destruyeron.
