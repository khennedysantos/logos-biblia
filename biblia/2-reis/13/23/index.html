2 Reis 13:23

Mas o SENHOR teve misericórdia deles, e compadeceu-se deles, e olhou-os, por amor de seu pacto com Abraão, Isaque e Jacó; e não quis destruí-los nem lançá-los de diante de si até agora.

KJV And the Lord was gracious unto them, and had compassion on them, and had respect unto them, because of his covenant with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and would not destroy them, neither cast he them from his presence as yet.

RVA Mas Jehová tuvo misericordia de ellos, y compadecióse de ellos, y mirólos, por amor de su pacto con Abraham, Isaac y Jacob; y no quiso destruirlos ni echarlos de delante de sí hasta ahora.
