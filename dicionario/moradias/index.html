Moradias

Easton · em inglês: Dwellings

Os materiais usados nas construções eram comumente tijolos, às vezes também pedras (Levítico 14:40, 42), que eram unidos por argamassa (Jeremias 43:9) ou betume (Gênesis 11:3). O exterior costumava ser caiado (Levítico 14:41; Ezequiel 13:10; Mateus 23:27). As vigas eram de sicômoro (Isaías 9:10), ou de madeira de oliveira, ou de cedro (1 Reis 7:2; Isaías 9:10).

Texto original (inglês)

The materials used in buildings were commonly bricks, sometimes also stones (Lev. 14:40, 42), which were held together by cement (Jer. 43:9) or bitumen (Gen. 11:3). The exterior was usually whitewashed (Lev. 14:41; Ezek. 13:10; Matt. 23:27). The beams were of sycamore (Isa. 9:10), or olive-wood, or cedar (1 Kings 7:2; Isa. 9:10).
