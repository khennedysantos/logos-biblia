Texto original (inglês)

An event recorded in Gen. 7 and 8. (See [216]DELUGE.)

In Josh. 24:2, 3, 14, 15, the word "flood" (R.V., "river") means the river Euphrates. In Ps. 66:6, this word refers to the river Jordan.
