Ásia

Easton · em inglês: Asia

É usado para designar a Ásia Proconsular, província romana que abrangia as partes ocidentais da Ásia Menor, da qual Éfeso era a capital, em Atos 2:9; 6:9; 16:6; 19:10, 22; 20:4, 16, 18, etc., e provavelmente a Ásia Menor em Atos 19:26, 27; 21:27; 24:18; 27:2. A Ásia Proconsular continha as sete igrejas do Apocalipse (Apocalipse 1:11). Os “principais da Ásia” (Atos 19:31) eram certos cidadãos ricos que eram anualmente eleitos para presidir aos jogos e festas religiosas das diversas cidades a que pertenciam. Alguns desses “asiarcas” eram amigos de Paulo.

Texto original (inglês)

Is used to denote Proconsular Asia, a Roman province which embraced the western parts of Asia Minor, and of which Ephesus was the capital, in Acts 2:9; 6:9; 16:6; 19:10, 22; 20:4, 16, 18, etc., and probably Asia Minor in Acts 19:26, 27; 21:27; 24:18; 27:2. Proconsular Asia contained the seven churches of the Apocalypse (Rev. 1:11). The "chiefs of Asia" (Acts 19:31) were certain wealthy citizens who were annually elected to preside over the games and religious festivals of the several cities to which they belonged. Some of these "Asiarchs" were Paul's friends.
