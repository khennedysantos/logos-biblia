Makkedah

Easton

Lugar de pastor, uma das cidades reais dos cananeus (Josué 12:16), perto da qual havia uma caverna onde os cinco reis que haviam conspirado contra Israel buscaram refúgio (10:10-29). Foram mortos por Josué, que depois pendurou seus corpos em cinco árvores. Foi identificada com a moderna aldeia chamada Sumeil, situada numa pequena colina cerca de 7 milhas a noroeste de Eleutrópolis (Beit Jibrin), onde há ruínas antigas e uma grande caverna. Os levantadores da Palestine Exploration Fund, entretanto, a identificaram com el-Mughar, ou “as cavernas”, a 3 milhas de Jabneel e 2 1/2 a sudoeste de Ecrom, porque, segundo dizem, “somente neste local, entre todos os locais possíveis para Maquedá na planície da Palestina, ainda existem cavernas”. (Veja ADONI-ZEDEQUE.)

Texto original (inglês)

Herdsman's place, one of the royal cities of the Canaanites (Josh. 12:16), near which was a cave where the five kings who had confederated against Israel sought refuge (10:10-29). They were put to death by Joshua, who afterwards suspended their bodies upon five trees. It has been identified with the modern village called Sumeil, standing on a low hill about 7 miles to the north-west of Eleutheropolis (Beit Jibrin), where are ancient remains and a great cave. The Palestine Exploration surveyors have, however, identified it with el-Mughar, or "the caves," 3 miles from Jabneh and 2 1/2 southwest of Ekron, because, they say, "at this site only of all possible sites for Makkedah in the Palestine plain do caves still exist." (See [373]ADONI-ZEDEC.)
