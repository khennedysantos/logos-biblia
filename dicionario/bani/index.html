Texto original (inglês)

Built. (1.) 1 Chr. 6:46.

(2.) One of David's thirty-seven warriors, a Gadite (2 Sam. 23:36).

(3.) Ezra 2:10; 10:29, 34, 38.

(4.) A Levite who was prominent in the reforms on the return from Babylon (Neh. 8:7; 9:4, 5). His son Rehum took part in rebuilding the wall of Jerusalem (Neh. 3:17).
