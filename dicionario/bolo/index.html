Bolo

Easton · em inglês: Cake

Bolos feitos de trigo ou cevada eram oferecidos no templo. Eram salgados, mas sem fermento (Êxodo 29:2; Levítico 2:4). No culto idólatra, bolos finos ou obreias eram oferecidos “à rainha do céu” (Jeremias 7:18; 44:19).

Texto original (inglês)

Cakes made of wheat or barley were offered in the temple. They were salted, but unleavened (Ex. 29:2; Lev. 2:4). In idolatrous worship thin cakes or wafers were offered "to the queen of heaven" (Jer. 7:18; 44:19).
