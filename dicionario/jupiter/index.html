Júpiter

Easton · em inglês: Jupiter

A principal divindade dos antigos gregos e romanos. Era adorado por eles sob vários epítetos. Barnabé foi identificado com este deus pelos licaônios (Atos 14:12), por causa de sua presença majestosa e imponente, pois assim supunham ser Júpiter. Havia um templo dedicado a este deus fora das portas de Listra (14:13).

Texto original (inglês)

The principal deity of the ancient Greeks and Romans. He was worshipped by them under various epithets. Barnabas was identified with this god by the Lycaonians (Acts 14:12), because he was of stately and commanding presence, as they supposed Jupiter to be. There was a temple dedicated to this god outside the gates of Lystra (14:13).
