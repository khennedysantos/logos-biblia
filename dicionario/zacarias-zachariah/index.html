Zacarias

Easton · em inglês: Zachariah

Lembrado pelo Senhor. (1.) Filho de Jeroboão II, rei de Israel. Com a morte de seu pai, houve um interregno de dez anos, ao fim do qual ele subiu ao trono, que ocupou por apenas seis meses, sendo morto por Salum, que usurpou o trono. “Fez o que era mau aos olhos do Senhor, como fizeram seus pais” (2 Reis 14:29; 15:8-12). Nele chegou ao fim a dinastia de Jeú.

(2.) Pai de Abi, que foi a mãe de Ezequias (2 Reis 18:2).

Texto original (inglês)

Remembered by the Lord. (1.) Son of Jeroboam II., king of Israel. On the death of his father there was an interregnum of ten years, at the end of which he succeeded to the throne, which he occupied only six months, having been put to death by Shallum, who usurped the throne. "He did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, as his fathers had done" (2 Kings 14:29; 15:8-12). In him the dynasty of Jehu came to an end.

(2.) The father of Abi, who was the mother of Hezekiah (2 Kings 18:2).
