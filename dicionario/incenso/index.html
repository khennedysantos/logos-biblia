Incenso

Easton · em inglês: Incense

Composição aromática preparada pela “arte do perfumista”. Consistia em quatro ingredientes “triturados finamente” (Êxodo 30:34-36). O que não era assim preparado era chamado de “incenso estranho” (30:9). Era oferecido juntamente com cada oferta de cereais e, além disso, era queimado diariamente sobre o altar de ouro no lugar santo e, no grande dia da expiação, era queimado pelo sumo sacerdote no santo dos santos (30:7, 8). Era símbolo da oração (Salmos 141:1, 2; Apocalipse 5:8; 8:3, 4).

Texto original (inglês)

A fragrant composition prepared by the "art of the apothecary." It consisted of four ingredients "beaten small" (Ex. 30:34-36). That which was not thus prepared was called "strange incense" (30:9). It was offered along with every meat-offering; and besides was daily offered on the golden altar in the holy place, and on the great day of atonement was burnt by the high priest in the holy of holies (30:7, 8). It was the symbol of prayer (Ps. 141:1, 2; Rev. 5:8; 8:3, 4).
