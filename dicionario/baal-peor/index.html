Baal-peor

Easton

Senhor da abertura, um deus dos moabitas (Números 25:3; 31:16; Josué 22:17), adorado com ritos obscenos. Assim chamado a partir do Monte Peor, onde esse culto era celebrado, o Baal de Peor. Os israelitas caíram no culto a esse ídolo (Números 25:3, 5, 18; Deuteronômio 4:3; Salmos 106:28; Oseias 9:10).

Texto original (inglês)

Lord of the opening, a god of the Moabites (Num. 25:3; 31:16; Josh. 22:17), worshipped by obscene rites. So called from Mount Peor, where this worship was celebrated, the Baal of Peor. The Israelites fell into the worship of this idol (Num. 25:3, 5, 18; Deut. 4:3; Ps. 106:28; Hos. 9:10).
