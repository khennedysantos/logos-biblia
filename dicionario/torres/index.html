Torres

Easton · em inglês: Towers

De Babel (Gênesis 11:4), Éder (Gênesis 35:21), Penuel (Juízes 8:9, 17), Siquém (9:46), Davi (Cantares 4:4), Líbano (7:4), Syene (Ezequiel 29:10), Hananeel (Zacarias 14:10), Siloé (Lucas 13:4). Havia várias torres em Jerusalém (2 Crônicas 26:9; Salmos 48:12). Eram erguidas para diversos fins, como torres de vigia em vinhedos (Isaías 5:2; Mateus 21:33) e torres de defesa.

Texto original (inglês)

Of Babel (Gen. 11:4), Edar (Gen. 35:21), Penuel (Judg. 8:9, 17), Shechem (9:46), David (Cant. 4:4), Lebanon (7:4), Syene (Ezek. 29:10), Hananeel (Zech. 14:10), Siloam (Luke 13:4). There were several towers in Jerusalem (2 Chr. 26:9; Ps. 48:12). They were erected for various purposes, as watch-towers in vineyard (Isa. 5:2; Matt. 21:33) and towers for defence.
