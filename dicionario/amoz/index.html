Amoz

Easton

Forte; pai do profeta Isaías (2 Reis 19:2, 20; 20:1; Isaías 1:1; 2:1). Quanto à sua história pessoal, pouco se sabe com certeza. Alguns supõem que tenha sido o “homem de Deus” mencionado em 2 Crônicas 25:7, 8.

Texto original (inglês)

Strong, the father of the prophet Isaiah (2 Kings 19:2, 20; 20:1; Isa. 1:1; 2:1). As to his personal history little is positively known. He is supposed by some to have been the "man of God" spoken of in 2 Chr. 25:7, 8.
