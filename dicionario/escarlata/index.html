Escarlata

Easton · em inglês: Scarlet

Este corante era obtido pelos egípcios do molusco Carthamus tinctorius; e pelos hebreus do Coccus ilicis, inseto que infesta carvalhos, chamado kermes pelos árabes.

Texto original (inglês)

This dye was obtained by the Egyptians from the shell-fish Carthamus tinctorius; and by the Hebrews from the Coccus ilicis, an insect which infests oak trees, called kermes by the Arabians.
