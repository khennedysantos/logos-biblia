Oferta de madeira

Easton · em inglês: Wood-offering

(Neemias 10:34; 13:31). Parece que, na época de Neemias, foram feitos arranjos, provavelmente por causa da relativa escassez de lenha, pelos quais certos distritos eram obrigados, por sorteio, a fornecer lenha para manter perpetuamente aceso o fogo do altar (Levítico 6:13).

Texto original (inglês)

(Neh. 10:34; 13:31). It would seem that in the time of Nehemiah arrangements were made, probably on account of the comparative scarcity of wood, by which certain districts were required, as chosen by lot, to furnish wood to keep the altar fire perpetually burning (Lev. 6:13).
