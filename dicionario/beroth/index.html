"Beroth"

Easton · em inglês: Betroth

Prometer “pela verdade de alguém”. Homens e mulheres eram desposados quando se comprometiam a casar-se. Isso geralmente acontecia um ano ou mais antes do casamento. A partir do noivado, a mulher era considerada a legítima esposa do homem com quem estava desposada (Deuteronômio 28:30; Juízes 14:2, 8; Mateus 1:18-21).

O termo é empregado figuradamente para expressar a conexão espiritual entre Deus e o seu povo (Oseias 2:19, 20).

Texto original (inglês)

To promise "by one's truth." Men and women were betrothed when they were engaged to be married. This usually took place a year or more before marriage. From the time of betrothal the woman was regarded as the lawful wife of the man to whom she was betrothed (Deut. 28:30; Judg. 14:2, 8; Matt. 1:18-21).

The term is figuratively employed of the spiritual connection between God and his people (Hos. 2:19, 20).
