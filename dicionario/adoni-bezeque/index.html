Adoni-Bezeque

Easton · em inglês: Adonibezek

Senhor de Bezeque, rei cananeu que, tendo subjugado setenta dos chefes ao seu redor, atacou os exércitos de Judá e Simeão, mas foi derrotado e levado cativo a Jerusalém, onde tiveram cortados os polegares das mãos e os dedões dos pés. Ele confessou que Deus lhe havia retribuído por sua semelhante crueldade para com os setenta reis que havia subjugado (Juízes 1:4-7; comp. 1 Samuel 15:33).

Texto original (inglês)

Lord of Bezek, a Canaanitish king who, having subdued seventy of the chiefs that were around him, made an attack against the armies of Judah and Simeon, but was defeated and brought as a captive to Jerusalem, where his thumbs and great toes were cut off. He confessed that God had requited him for his like cruelty to the seventy kings whom he had subdued (Judg. 1:4-7; comp. 1 Sam. 15:33).
