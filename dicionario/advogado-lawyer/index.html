Advogado

Easton · em inglês: Lawyer

Entre os judeus, era aquele versado nas leis de Moisés, as quais expunha nas escolas e sinagogas (Mateus 22:35; Lucas 10:25). As funções de “doutor da lei” e de “escriba” eram idênticas. (Veja DOUTOR.)

Texto original (inglês)

Among the Jews, was one versed in the laws of Moses, which he expounded in the schools and synagogues (Matt. 22:35; Luke 10:25). The functions of the "lawyer" and "scribe" were identical. (See [350]DOCTOR.)
