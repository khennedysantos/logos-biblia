Estéfanas

Easton · em inglês: Stephanas

Crispo, membro da igreja de Corinto, cuja família estava entre as que o apóstolo havia batizado (1 Coríntios 1:16; 16:15, 17). Alguns supõem que ele seria o “carcereiro de Filipos” (cf. Atos 16:33). A Primeira Epístola aos Coríntios foi escrita de Filipos cerca de seis anos após a conversão do carcereiro, e ele estava com o apóstolo ali naquela ocasião.

Texto original (inglês)

Crown, a member of the church at Corinth, whose family were among those the apostle had baptized (1 Cor. 1:16; 16:15, 17). He has been supposed by some to have been the "jailer of Philippi" (comp. Acts 16:33). The First Epistle to the Corinthians was written from Philippi some six years after the jailer's conversion, and he was with the apostle there at that time.
