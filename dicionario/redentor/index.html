Heb. goel; isto é, aquele encarregado do dever de restaurar os direitos de outro e vingar suas injustiças (Levítico 25:48, 49; Números 5:8; Rute 4:1; Jó 19:25; Salmos 19:14; 78:35, etc.). Texto original (inglês)

Heb. goel; i.e., one charged with the duty of restoring the rights of another and avenging his wrongs (Lev. 25:48, 49; Num. 5:8; Ruth 4:1; Job 19:25; Ps. 19:14; 78:35, etc.). This title is peculiarly applied to Christ. He redeems us from all evil by the payment of a ransom (q.v.). (See [524]REDEMPTION.)
