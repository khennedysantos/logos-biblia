Baal-Tamar

Easton

Senhor das palmeiras, um lugar na tribo de Benjamim, perto de Geba de Saul (Juízes 20:33). Era um dos santuários ou bosques de Baal. Provavelmente a palmeira de Débora (Juízes 4:5) seja aludida no nome.

Texto original (inglês)

Lord of palm trees, a place in the tribe of Benjamin near Gibeah of Saul (Judg. 20:33). It was one of the sanctuaries or groves of Baal. Probably the palm tree of Deborah (Judg. 4:5) is alluded to in the name.
