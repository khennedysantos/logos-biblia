Serva

Easton · em inglês: Handmaid

Serva (Gênesis 16:1; Rute 3:9; Lucas 1:48). É provável que Hagar fosse a criada pessoal de Sarai enquanto esta estava na casa do faraó, e que estivesse entre as servas que Abrão trouxera do Egito.

Texto original (inglês)

Servant (Gen. 16:1; Ruth 3:9; Luke 1:48). It is probable that Hagar was Sarah's personal attendant while she was in the house of Pharaoh, and was among those maid-servants whom Abram had brought from Egypt.
