Siclo

Easton · em inglês: Shekel

Peso, o padrão comum tanto de peso quanto de valor entre os hebreus. Estima-se em 220 grãos ingleses, ou um pouco mais de meia onça avoirdupois. O “siclo do santuário” (Êxodo 30:13; Números 3:47) equivalia a vinte geras (Ezequiel 45:12). Havia siclos de ouro (1 Crônicas 21:25), de prata (1 Samuel 9:8), de bronze (17:5) e de ferro (7). Quando passou a ser uma moeda cunhada, o siclo de ouro equivalia a cerca de 2 libras da nossa moeda. Seis siclos de ouro, segundo o sistema judaico posterior, valiam o mesmo que cinquenta siclos de prata.

Texto original (inglês)

Weight, the common standard both of weight and value among the Hebrews. It is estimated at 220 English grains, or a little more than half an ounce avoirdupois. The "shekel of the sanctuary" (Ex. 30:13; Num. 3:47) was equal to twenty gerahs (Ezek. 45:12). There were shekels of gold (1 Chr. 21:25), of silver (1 Sam. 9:8), of brass (17:5), and of iron (7). When it became a coined piece of money, the shekel of gold was equivalent to about 2 pound of our money. Six gold shekels, according to the later Jewish system, were equal in value to fifty silver ones.
