Tyrannus

Easton

Príncipe, um retórico grego, em cuja “escola” em Éfeso Paulo debatia diariamente, durante o espaço de dois anos, com os que vinham a ele (Atos 19:9). Alguns supuseram que ele era judeu, e que sua “escola” era uma sinagoga particular.

Texto original (inglês)

Prince, a Greek rhetorician, in whose "school" at Ephesus Paul disputed daily for the space of two years with those who came to him (Acts 19:9). Some have supposed that he was a Jew, and that his "school" was a private synagogue.
