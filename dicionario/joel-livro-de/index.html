Joel, Livro de

Easton · em inglês: Joel, Book of

Joel provavelmente residia em Judá, pois sua missão era dirigida àquele povo. Ele faz menção frequente a Judá e a Jerusalém (1:14; 2:1, 15, 32; 3:1, 12, 17, 20, 21).

Texto original (inglês)

Joel was probably a resident in Judah, as his commission was to that people. He makes frequent mention of Judah and Jerusalem (1:14; 2:1, 15, 32; 3:1, 12, 17, 20, 21).
