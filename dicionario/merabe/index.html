Merabe

Easton · em inglês: Merab

Aumento; a mais velha das duas filhas de Saul (1 Samuel 14:49). Foi prometida em casamento a Davi após a vitória sobre Golias, mas não parece ter aderido de bom grado a esse arranjo (18:2, 17, 19). Por fim, casou-se com Adriel, de Abel-Meolá, cidade do vale do Jordão, cerca de 16 quilômetros ao sul de Bete-Sã, com quem a casa de Saul manteve aliança. Teve cinco filhos, todos entregues à morte pelos gibeonitas no monte de Gibeá (2 Samuel 21:8).

Texto original (inglês)

Increase, the eldest of Saul's two daughters (1 Sam. 14:49). She was betrothed to David after his victory over Goliath, but does not seem to have entered heartily into this arrangement (18:2, 17, 19). She was at length, however, married to Adriel of Abel-Meholah, a town in the Jordan valley, about 10 miles south of Bethshean, with whom the house of Saul maintained alliance. She had five sons, who were all put to death by the Gibeonites on the hill of Gibeah (2 Sam. 21:8).
